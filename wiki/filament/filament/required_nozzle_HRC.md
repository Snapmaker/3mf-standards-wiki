## Required nozzle HRC

[Mode](../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Developer`.  
[Variable](../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `required_nozzle_HRC`.  
[Type](../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Integer list`.  
[CLI Example](../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--required-nozzle-HRC=1`.  
Minimum HRC of nozzle required to print the filament. A value of 0 means no checking of the nozzle's HRC.
