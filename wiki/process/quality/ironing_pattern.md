> Location: Process → Quality → Ironing

---

## Pattern

[Mode](../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `ironing_pattern`.  
[Type](../../general_settings/option_type.md#choice): `Choice`.  
[Options](../../general_settings/option_type.md#choice): `rectilinear, concentric`.  
[CLI Example](../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--ironing-pattern=rectilinear`.  
The pattern that will be used when ironing. Usually, the best pattern is the one with the most efficient coverage of the surface.  

> [!TIP]
> See [Infill Patterns Wiki List](../../process/strength/strength_settings_patterns.md) with **detailed specifications**, including their strengths and weaknesses.

The ironing patterns are:

- **[Concentric](../../process/strength/strength_settings_patterns.md#concentric)**
- **[Rectilinear](../../process/strength/strength_settings_patterns.md#rectilinear)**
