# support_filament

> Location: Process → Support → Support filament

---

## Base

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Simple`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `support_filament`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Integer`.  
[Default](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#default-value): `0`.  
[Range](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#range): `≥ 0`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--support-filament=1`.  
Filament to print the support base and raft. "Default" means no specific filament for support, and the current filament is used.
