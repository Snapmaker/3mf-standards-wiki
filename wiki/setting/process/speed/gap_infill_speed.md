# gap_infill_speed

> Location: Process → Speed → Other layers speed

---

## Gap infill

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `gap_infill_speed`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Float list`.  
[Default](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#default-value): `30`.  
[Range](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#range): `≥ 1` mm/s.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--gap-infill-speed=1`.  
Speed of [gap infill](../../process/strength/gap_fill_target.md), which is used to fill small gaps or holes in the print.
