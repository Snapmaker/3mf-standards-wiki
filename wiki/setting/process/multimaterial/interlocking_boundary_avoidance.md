## Interlocking Boundary Avoidance

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `interlocking_boundary_avoidance`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Integer`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--interlocking-boundary-avoidance=1`.  
The distance from the outside of a model where interlocking structures will not be generated, measured in cells.
