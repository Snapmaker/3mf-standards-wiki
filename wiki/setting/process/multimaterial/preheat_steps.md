## Preheat steps

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Developer`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `preheat_steps`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Integer`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--preheat-steps=1`.  
Inserts multiple preheat commands (e.g. M104.1). Only useful for the Prusa XL. For other printers, set it to 1.
