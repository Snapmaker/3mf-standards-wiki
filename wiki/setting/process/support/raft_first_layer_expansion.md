## Initial layer expansion

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `raft_first_layer_expansion`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Float`.  
[Default](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#default-value): `-1`.  
[Range](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#range): `≥ -1` mm.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--raft-first-layer-expansion=1`.  
Expands the first raft or support layer to improve build plate adhesion. It is disabled for the Tree Slim and Tree Strong styles unless a raft is used.
