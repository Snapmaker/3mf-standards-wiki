## Wall loops

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Simple`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `wall_loops`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Integer`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--wall-loops=1`.  
"Wall loops" is the number of walls printed on each layer.  
Increasing the wall loops will:

- Enhance:
    - Layer adhesion
    - Strength
    - Rigidity
- Reduce infill ghosting
- Increase print time
