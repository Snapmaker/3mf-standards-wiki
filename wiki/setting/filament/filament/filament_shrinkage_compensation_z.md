> Location: Filament → Filament → Basic information

---

## Shrinkage (Z)

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `filament_shrinkage_compensation_z`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Percentage list`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--filament-shrinkage-compensation-z=20%`.  
Enter the shrinkage percentage that the filament will get after cooling (94% if you measure 94mm instead of 100mm). The part will be scaled in Z to compensate.
