> Location: Process → Strength → Advanced

---

## Infill Combination

[Mode](../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `infill_combination`.  
[Type](../../general_settings/option_type.md#boolean): `Boolean`.  
[CLI Example](../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--infill-combination=1`.  
Automatically combine [sparse infill](../../process/strength/strength_settings_infill.md) of several layers so they print together, reducing print time. Walls are still printed with the original [layer height](../../process/quality/quality_settings_layer_height.md).
