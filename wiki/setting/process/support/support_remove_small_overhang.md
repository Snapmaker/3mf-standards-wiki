# support_remove_small_overhang

> Location: Process → Support → Support

---

## Ignore small overhangs

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `support_remove_small_overhang`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#boolean): `Boolean`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--support-remove-small-overhang=1`.  
With this setting, small overhangs that possibly don't need support are ignored by support generation.
