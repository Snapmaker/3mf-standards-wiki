## Extra flow for purge

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `wipe_tower_extra_flow`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Percentage`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--wipe-tower-extra-flow=20%`.  
Extra flow used for the purging lines on the wipe tower. This makes the purging lines thicker than they normally would be. The spacing is adjusted automatically.
