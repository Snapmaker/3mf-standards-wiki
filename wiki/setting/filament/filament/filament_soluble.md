## Soluble material

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `filament_soluble`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Boolean list`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--filament-soluble=1`.  
Soluble material is commonly used to print supports and support interfaces.
