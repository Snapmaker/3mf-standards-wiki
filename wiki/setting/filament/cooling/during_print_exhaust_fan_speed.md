# during_print_exhaust_fan_speed

> Location: Filament → Cooling → Exhaust fan

---

#### During print

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Simple`.  
[Variables](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `activate_air_filtration_during_print`, `during_print_exhaust_fan_speed`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md): `activate_air_filtration_during_print` (Boolean list), `during_print_exhaust_fan_speed` (Integer list).  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--activate-air-filtration-during-print=1` (`activate_air_filtration_during_print` shown; other variables above follow their own type).  
Speed of exhaust fan during printing. This speed will override the speed in filament custom G-code.
