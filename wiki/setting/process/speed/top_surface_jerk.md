# top_surface_jerk

> Location: Process → Speed → Jerk(XY)

---

### Top surface

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `top_surface_jerk`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Float list`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--top-surface-jerk=1`.  
Jerk for top surface printing. This is usually set to a lower value than infill to ensure better quality.
