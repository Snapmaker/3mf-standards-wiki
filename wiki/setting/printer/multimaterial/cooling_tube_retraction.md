## Cooling tube position

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `cooling_tube_retraction`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Float`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--cooling-tube-retraction=1`.  
Distance of the center-point of the cooling tube from the extruder tip.
