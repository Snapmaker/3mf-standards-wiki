# machine_tool_change_time

> Location: Printer → Multimaterial → Advanced

---

## Tool change time

[Mode](../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `machine_tool_change_time`.  
[Type](../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Float`.  
[CLI Example](../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--machine-tool-change-time=1`.  
Time taken to switch tools. It's usually applicable for tool changers or multi-tool machines. For single-extruder multi-material machines, it's typically 0. For statistics only.
