# filament_cooling_final_speed

> Location: Filament → Multimaterial → Tool change parameters with single extruder MM printers

---

### Speed of the last cooling move

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `filament_cooling_final_speed`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Float list`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--filament-cooling-final-speed=1`.  
Cooling moves are gradually accelerating towards this speed.
