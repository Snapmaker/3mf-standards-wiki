# filament_unloading_speed_start

> Location: Filament → Multimaterial → Tool change parameters with single extruder MM printers

---

### Unloading speed at the start

[Mode](../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `filament_unloading_speed_start`.  
[Type](../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Float list`.  
[CLI Example](../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--filament-unloading-speed-start=1`.  
Speed used for unloading the tip of the filament immediately after ramming.
