## Support/object XY distance

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `support_object_xy_distance`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Float`.  
[Default](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#default-value): `0.35`.  
[Range](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#range): `0–10` mm.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--support-object-xy-distance=1`.  
XY separation between an object and its support.
