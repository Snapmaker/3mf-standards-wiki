# travel_slope

> Location: Printer → Extruder N → Z-Hop

---

## Traveling angle

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `travel_slope[extruder_idx]`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Float list`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--travel-slope=1`.  
Traveling angle for Slope and Spiral Z-hop type. Setting it to 90° results in Normal Lift.
