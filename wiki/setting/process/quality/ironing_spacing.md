# ironing_spacing

> Location: Process → Quality → Ironing

---

## Line spacing

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `ironing_spacing`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Float`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--ironing-spacing=1`.  
The distance between the lines of ironing.  
It's recommended to set this value to be equal to or less than the nozzle diameter for optimal coverage and surface finish.
