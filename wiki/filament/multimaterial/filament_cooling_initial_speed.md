# filament_cooling_initial_speed

> Location: Filament → Multimaterial → Tool change parameters with single extruder MM printers

---

### Speed of the first cooling move

[Mode](../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `filament_cooling_initial_speed`.  
[Type](../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Float list`.  
[CLI Example](../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--filament-cooling-initial-speed=1`.  
Cooling moves are gradually accelerating beginning at this speed.
