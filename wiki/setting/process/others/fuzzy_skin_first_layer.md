> Location: Process → Others → Fuzzy Skin

## Apply fuzzy skin to first layer

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Simple`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `fuzzy_skin_first_layer`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#boolean): `Boolean`.  
[Default](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#default-value): `0`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--fuzzy-skin-first-layer=1`.  
Enable to apply fuzzy skin to the first layer.

> [!CAUTION]
> Can impact bed adhesion and surface contact.
