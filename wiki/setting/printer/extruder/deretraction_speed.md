# deretraction_speed

> Location: Printer → Extruder N → Retraction

---

## Deretraction speed

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `deretraction_speed[extruder_idx]`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Float list`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--deretraction-speed=1`.  
Speed for reloading filament into the nozzle. Zero means same speed of retraction.
