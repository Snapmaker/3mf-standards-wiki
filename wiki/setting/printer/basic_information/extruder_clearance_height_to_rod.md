# extruder_clearance_height_to_rod

> Location: Printer → Basic information → Extruder Clearance

---

## Height to rod

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `extruder_clearance_height_to_rod`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Float`.  
[Default](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#default-value): `40`.  
[Range](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#range): `≥ 0` mm.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--extruder-clearance-height-to-rod=1`.  
Distance from the nozzle tip to the lower rod. Used for collision avoidance in by-object printing.
