#### Stabilization cone apex angle

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `wipe_tower_cone_angle`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Float`.  
[Default](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#default-value): `30.0`.  
[Unit](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#unit): `°`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--wipe-tower-cone-angle=1`.  

Angle at the apex of the cone that is used to stabilize the wipe tower. A larger angle means a wider base.
