## Preferred orientation

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `preferred_orientation`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Float`.  
[Default](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#default-value): `0.0`.  
[Range](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#range): `-360–360` °.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--preferred-orientation=1`.  
Automatically orient STL files on the Z axis upon initial import.
