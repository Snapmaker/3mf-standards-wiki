# support_line_width

> Location: Process → Quality → Line width

---

### Support

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `support_line_width`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Float or Percentage`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--support-line-width=20%`.  
Typically set to **100%** to balance material usage and functionality. Reducing it too much can lead to weak support structures that may not hold up during printing or break easily during removal, leaving debris on the model.
