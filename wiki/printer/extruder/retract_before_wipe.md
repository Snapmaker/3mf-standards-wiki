# retract_before_wipe

> Location: Printer → Extruder N → Retraction

---

## Retract amount before wipe

[Mode](../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `retract_before_wipe[extruder_idx]`.  
[Type](../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Percentage list`.  
[CLI Example](../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--retract-before-wipe=20%`.  
This is the length of fast retraction before a wipe, relative to retraction length.
