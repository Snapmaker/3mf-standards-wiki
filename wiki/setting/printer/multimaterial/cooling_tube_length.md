## Cooling tube length

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `cooling_tube_length`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Float`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--cooling-tube-length=1`.  
Length of the cooling tube to limit space for cooling moves inside it.
