## Branch Angle

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variables](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `tree_support_branch_angle`, `tree_support_branch_angle_organic`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Float`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--tree-support-branch-angle=1` (same pattern for the other variables above).  
This setting determines the maximum overhang angle that the branches of tree support are allowed to make. If the angle is increased, the branches can be printed more horizontally, allowing them to reach farther.
