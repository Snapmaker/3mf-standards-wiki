## Layer change G-code

[Mode](../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `layer_change_gcode`.  
[Type](../../general_settings/option_type.md#text): `Text`.  
[CLI Example](../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--layer-change-gcode=value`.  
This G-code is inserted at every layer change after the Z lift.
