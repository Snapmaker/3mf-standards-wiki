# extruder_clearance_height_to_lid

> Location: Printer → Basic information → Extruder Clearance

---

## Height to lid

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `extruder_clearance_height_to_lid`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#integer-float-percentage): `Float`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--extruder-clearance-height-to-lid=1`.  
Distance from the nozzle tip to the lid. Used for collision avoidance in by-object printing.
