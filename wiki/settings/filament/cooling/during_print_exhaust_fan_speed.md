# during_print_exhaust_fan_speed

> Location: Filament → Cooling → Exhaust fan

---

#### During print

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Simple`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `during_print_exhaust_fan_speed`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md): Integer list.  
[Default](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#default-value): `60`.  
[Range](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#range): `0–100` %.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--during-print-exhaust-fan-speed=1`.  
Speed of exhaust fan during printing. This speed will override the speed in filament custom G-code.
