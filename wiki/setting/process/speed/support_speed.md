# support_speed

> Location: Process → Speed → Other layers speed

---

## Support

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Advanced`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `support_speed`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Float list`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--support-speed=1`.  
Speed at which [support](../../process/support/support_settings_support.md) material is printed. Slower speeds help ensure that supports are stable and effective during the print process.
