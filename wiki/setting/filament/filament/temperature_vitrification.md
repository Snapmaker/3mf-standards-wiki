## Softening temperature

[Mode](../../../general_settings/option_mode.md): `Simple`.  
[Variable](../../../developer_reference/built_in_placeholders_variables.md): `temperature_vitrification`.  
[Type](../../../general_settings/option_type.md#list-types): `Integer list`.  
[CLI Example](../../../cli/cli_mode.md#setting-overrides): `--temperature-vitrification=1`.  
The material softens at this temperature, so when the bed temperature is equal to or greater than this, it's highly recommended to open the front door and/or remove the upper glass to avoid clogs.
